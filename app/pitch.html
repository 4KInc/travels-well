Pitch Desk

Your slate · their catalogue · what to lead with

You have twenty minutes and five slots. Describe a buyer by what they have already licensed, and this ranks your own titles for them. Your strongest domestic titles are not necessarily your strongest pitch to a particular buyer — the shortlist is ordered by how well each title fits them, with its movement against your home audience shown beside it.

A buyer's catalogue of six titles or more is split into halves, each half read separately, and any of your titles the two halves rank very differently is withheld rather than reported. Titles appearing in both your slate and a describing catalogue are removed and named: Qloo excludes an entity from its own results, so they cannot be scored against themselves.
