What we measured

Thresholds were written down and committed before any data was collected, and every amendment is recorded rather than edited in. Everything below is reproducible from the repository with run_all.py.

The API, measured on the hackathon endpoint

These are properties of the endpoint as we found it, verified by differential testing rather than read from documentation. A 200 response is not evidence that a parameter was applied.

QuestionMeasured
filter.location.query localises results Not for movies. Affinities identical to four decimals across New York, São Paulo, Tokyo, Mumbai and Berlin. It is honoured for places, where Bronx, Manhattan and Brooklyn return genuinely distinct venues.
Affinity normalised per query Not per candidate set. The same entity scored inside two different pinned sets with the same signal returns an identical value. It varies with the signal, not the set.
Which field is the taste score query.affinity. popularity sits beside it and is identical in every market. Our own client read it at first, which would have made every audience look the same.
Cross-domain signal strength TV shows 0.06, video games 0.08, music 0.11 discriminate film taste sharply. Brands 0.39, books 0.70, podcasts 0.72 barely. Places 0.67–0.98, not at all. Anchors are drawn only from the three that work.
Neighbourhood-level taste Unavailable. Venue sets differ by borough; the films that follow are the same prestige cluster. Restaurants do not predict film taste.
take Capped at 50. A larger value returns a 400 that reads downstream as “no data”.

What the map does not claim

The eighteen audiences are clusters of cultural anchors grouped by modelled film affinity — communities within Qloo’s graph, not observed groups of people. Fifteen of the eighteen have too few members to split into halves that can disagree, and are drawn dashed on the map and labelled single-reading in the panel.

Reproduce any of this: python3 run_all.py for the API harness, atlas_build.py for the clustering and bootstrap, check_anchor_stability.py for the anchor-swap result, and backtest_outcomes.py for the pre-registered outcome data. The pre-registration and both amendments are committed under out/.
